Mouse Touch – Privacy Policy

App: Mouse Touch (package com.kremlworld.mousetouch)
Developer: KremlWorld
Effective date: November 9, 2026

Summary

Mouse Touch does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or sensitive data. The app has no internet access: nothing it handles ever leaves your device.

What Mouse Touch does

Mouse Touch turns mouse clicks and drags into touch taps and swipes on the screen under the cursor (the phone's screen, an external monitor, or Samsung DeX), so games and apps that ignore a mouse respond to it. The scroll wheel performs a pinch-to-zoom gesture, and a right-click acts as Back.

Use of the Accessibility Service API

To provide this feature, Mouse Touch uses Android's Accessibility Service API. Mouse Touch is not an accessibility tool for people with disabilities; it uses this API only for the mouse-to-touch feature described above. Before the service can be turned on, the app shows a disclosure explaining this and asks for your explicit consent. If the service is turned on without that consent, it stays inactive.

Through the Accessibility Service API, Mouse Touch:

  • Receives mouse input (button presses, scroll-wheel movement, cursor position and movement) while it is active. When you pause it, it stops receiving mouse input and the mouse works normally.
  • Performs gestures (taps, swipes and two-finger pinch-to-zoom gestures at the cursor position, and the Back action for right-clicks) on your behalf.

Mouse Touch also reads basic display information (which screens are connected and their sizes) through standard Android APIs, so taps go to the screen the cursor is on.

Mouse Touch does not read, record or capture screen content, the contents of other apps, text you type, passwords, notifications, or any other information shown on your device.

All of this is processed in real time on your device and immediately discarded. None of it is saved, except as described under “Diagnostic log” below.

Data collection and sharing

  • Mouse Touch does not collect personal or sensitive user data.
  • Mouse Touch does not share any data with third parties.
  • Mouse Touch contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • Mouse Touch does not request internet access, so it cannot send data off your device.

Diagnostic log

To help with troubleshooting, Mouse Touch keeps a short text log in its private storage on your device. It contains only technical status messages, such as when the service starts or stops, display numbers and sizes, and which screen taps are sent to. It contains no personal information, no screen content and no record of what you click on. The log never leaves your device unless you choose to copy it yourself. You can view and clear it at any time in Mouse Touch → Troubleshooting, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Settings stored on your device

Mouse Touch stores a few settings in its private storage: whether it is active or paused, your screen-target choice, and whether you have agreed to the disclosure. These are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your choices

  • Pause Mouse Touch at any time from the app or its Quick Settings tile.
  • Turn the service off in Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps → Mouse Touch.
  • Review or withdraw your consent in Mouse Touch → Troubleshooting → Permission & privacy disclosure.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its stored settings and its diagnostic log.

Children

Mouse Touch does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a change affects how the Accessibility Service API is used, the app will ask for your consent again.
